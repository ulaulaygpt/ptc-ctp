Governing principle

The package is a transport, preservation, and reconstruction container. It is not by itself the protected cryptographic object and does not replace the authority of the source file and canonical object with PTC.

Document map

PKG-02 defines which pieces travel together, the authority of each, how they are located, how their coherence is checked, and what a verifier must do when faced with contradictions or risks.

PART I · Scope and authority

1. Purpose

PKG-02 defines an interoperable ZIP format for transporting an exact source, its FRS-01 reference, a PTC, derived copies, routing metadata, and, optionally, public-disclosure materials.

The specification is intended to allow a third party to find the pieces, verify each axis separately, detect internal contradictions, and reconstruct derived copies without blindly trusting the manifest.

2. Scope

Minimum and extended structure of package 2.0.

Roles and authority hierarchy of each artefact.

Grammar and semantics of manifest.json.

Exact relationships between the integrated record and derived copies.

Generation, verification, and reconstruction.

Structural conformance of the package as an independent axis.

Security rules for ZIP paths, entries, and resources.

Compatibility with earlier packages and repackaging.

Interoperability, contradiction, and hostile-treatment vectors.

3. Out of scope

Redefining R-01, PTC-F, ARCH, or FRS-01.

Defining the normative content of disclosure-notice-v1.

Turning the outer ZIP hash into the cryptographic root of the system.

Asserting operator identity, temporal priority, or legal validity.

Interpreting the internal content of the source.

Guaranteeing unlimited extraction or processing of files of any size on any device.

Fixing a hosting service or public URL.

5. Normative vocabulary

MUST and MUST NOT express conformance requirements; SHOULD and SHOULD NOT express recommendations that require justification to depart from; MAY expresses a permitted option.

Precedence rule

If manifest.json contradicts the integrated record or source, the manifest is wrong. If a derived copy contradicts the integrated record, the derived copy is wrong. The contradiction affects package conformance but does not rewrite the authoritative artefact.

PART II · Package structure

8. Defined profile and class

PKG-02 defines complete packages for input_adapter equal to file-reference-sha256-v1. A separate FRS record without the source may remain useful in accordance with FRS-01, but it does not constitute a complete package 2.0.

9. Mandatory minimum structure

manifest.json
README.txt
ptc.txt

source/
    <a single source file>

canonical/
    file_reference.txt
    file_reference_with_ptc.txt

Directories may exist as explicit entries or be implicit through file paths. Conformance is decided by file entries, not by the presence of ZIP directory entries.

A package 2.0 with GREEN status MUST NOT contain file entries other than the mandatory ones, those declared through public_notice, or those defined by a normative extension expressly recognised by the verifier.

A safe additional entry that is undeclared and has no normative function produces AMBER on the package axis and MUST NOT be silently ignored.

A dangerous additional entry, one that is ambiguous with respect to reserved roles, or one that contradicts an authoritative artefact produces RED on the package axis.

12. Path rules

All internal paths MUST use / as separator.

The names of all entries MUST be unambiguously decodable as UTF-8.

When a name contains non-ASCII characters, the ZIP format UTF-8 indicator (bit 11 of the general purpose bit flag) MUST be set. A non-ASCII name without that indicator produces RED on the package axis.

Reserved normative paths remain exclusively ASCII.

Paths MUST be relative and may not begin with /, \\, or a drive letter.

Empty segments, . and .. are not permitted after separator normalisation.

NUL and control characters U+0000–U+001F or U+007F are not permitted in entry names.

The reserved paths manifest.json, README.txt, ptc.txt, and canonical/ are case-sensitive and MUST appear exactly as shown.

There may not be more than one entry producing the same normalised path.

Collisions differing only in letter case are not permitted.

Symbolic links, hard links, and entries redirecting outside the ZIP are not permitted.

13. Single-source rule

A package 2.0 MUST contain exactly one file entry identified by source_path. That entry MUST be a direct descendant of source/ and may not be manifest.json, a canonical artefact, or presentation material.

The source MAY itself be a ZIP. The inner ZIP is the declared object; the outer ZIP is only the transport package.

14. Stored name and original name

The entry name in source/ may preserve a safe name or use a sanitised one. original_filename preserves the base name declared by the operator and is not part of the cryptographic identity.

original_filename MUST NOT contain path separators or control characters.

A name change without a byte change does not require regenerating the PTC.

A source_path change does require updating manifest.json, but does not modify the descriptor or its hashes.

PART III · manifest.json

15. Physical form

MUST be valid JSON encoded as UTF-8 without BOM.

MUST contain a JSON object at the root.

Comments, duplicate keys, and NaN/Infinity values are not permitted.

Path strings use / and must comply with section 12.

Key order and indentation are not normative.

A generator SHOULD emit LF and terminate the file with a single LF for reproducibility.

19. public_notice object

When disclosure exists, manifest.json MUST include public_notice. The key uses snake_case and is not the literal name of the PUBLIC_NOTICE/ folder.

{
  "public_notice": {
    "profile": "disclosure-notice-v1",
    "readme_path": "PUBLIC_NOTICE/README.txt",
    "notice_path": "PUBLIC_NOTICE/notice.txt",
    "html_path": "PUBLIC_NOTICE/notice.html",
    "svg_path": "PUBLIC_NOTICE/notice.svg",
    "qr_path": "PUBLIC_NOTICE/qr.svg",
    "verification_url": "https://example.org/declaration"
  }
}

public_notice MUST be omitted entirely when no disclosure exists.

profile MUST be disclosure-notice-v1.

readme_path, notice_path, html_path, and svg_path MUST be present.

qr_path and verification_url appear together or are omitted together.

verification_url MUST comply with disclosure-notice-v1 rules; PKG-02 only transports it.

The presence of public_notice in manifest.json and the presence of PUBLIC_NOTICE/ in the ZIP MUST be coherent. Their divergence constitutes a package incident and MUST NOT by itself alter the independent results of the PTC declaration or the source.

20. Unknown keys

An unknown key does not alter the PTC or the source. A verifier MUST ignore it for cryptographic verification, but SHOULD show a package incident so that a typographical error does not pass unnoticed.

21. The manifest is not a source of truth

The verifier uses manifest.json to route the process and then confirms every assertion against the actual pieces. input_adapter does not turn arbitrary text into FRS-01; source_path does not change the declared bytes; and original_filename or media_type do not modify identity.

PART IV · Generation, verification, and reconstruction

22. Generation algorithm

1. Finalise the final version of the source file.

2. Calculate size and source_sha256 in accordance with FRS-01.

3. Generate file_reference.txt in accordance with FRS-01.

4. Generate the PTC in accordance with R-01 and PTC-F.

5. Construct file_reference_with_ptc.txt by concatenating the canonical object and the PTC block according to R-01.

6. Extract the body and PTC block from the integrated record to produce the two derived copies.

7. Copy the source unmodified to source_path.

8. Generate manifest.json with the actual paths and informative metadata.

9. Generate README.txt.

10. If disclosure exists, generate the materials and public_notice in accordance with its specification.

11. Create the ZIP without encryption and apply the security and reproducibility rules.

Source stable during the process

The implementation MUST detect, where the environment permits, whether the source changes between calculation of its fingerprint and incorporation into the ZIP. On divergence, it must abort and start again.

23. Verification algorithm

1. Inspect the ZIP central directory before extraction and apply security limits.

2. Normalise and validate all paths; reject dangerous or ambiguous entries.

3. Locate manifest.json and check its form.

4. Locate the integrated record by its fixed path and extract the body and PTC according to R-01.

5. Verify the PTC and canonical object in accordance with R-01/PTC-F.

6. Confirm that the body satisfies FRS-01 and that input_adapter is coherent.

7. Compare the extracted body exactly with canonical/file_reference.txt.

8. Compare the extracted block exactly with ptc.txt.

9. Locate source_path and verify size and source_sha256 in accordance with FRS-01.

10. Check that all declared paths exist and point to the expected artefact type.

11. If public_notice exists, delegate its verification to disclosure-notice-v1.

12. Emit separate results for PTC, source, package, and disclosure.

24. Equality of derived copies

extract_body(canonical/file_reference_with_ptc.txt)
    == bytes(canonical/file_reference.txt)

extract_ptc(canonical/file_reference_with_ptc.txt)
    == bytes(ptc.txt)

Equality is byte-for-byte. Derived copies are not re-normalised to hide physical divergences. The integrated record already contains the applicable canonical forms.

PART V · Package conformance as a separate axis

27. Prohibition of a collapsed global verdict

Results MUST be shown in independent blocks. Package conformance MUST NOT overwrite the status of the PTC, source, or disclosure and MUST NOT be combined by a rule such as “the worst result prevails”.

PTC declaration:        GREEN / RED
Source file:            GREEN / AMBER / RED
Package structure:      GREEN / AMBER / RED
Public disclosure:      GREEN / AMBER / RED   # when present

28. Package-axis states

Status

Condition

Minimum message

GREEN

Mandatory entries present, safe paths, conformant manifest, and coherent copies.

The package 2.0 is structurally conformant.

AMBER

Recoverable defect: a derived copy, README, or manifest is missing; the core can be located or reconstructed.

The package has incidents but preserves verifiable elements.

AMBER

The device rejects a package due to local limits even though the package itself presents no structural violation.

It could not be fully processed on this device.

RED

Contradiction between integrated record and derived copies.

The package contains incompatible representations.

RED

Dangerous, duplicate, or ambiguous paths, encryption, or multiple manifests.

The package cannot be processed safely.

RED

The authoritative integrated record is missing.

It is not a complete package 2.0; a separate record may exist.

AMBER

A safe, undeclared additional entry with no normative function exists.

The package contains unrecognised additional material; the other axes remain separate.

RED

A dangerous additional entry exists, is ambiguous with respect to reserved roles, or is contradictory.

The package contains material preventing safe structural interpretation.

RED

A non-ASCII name is not unambiguously marked as UTF-8.

The path cannot be interpreted interoperably.

Example of asymmetry

A package may be RED because ptc.txt contradicts the integrated record, while the PTC declaration extracted from the integrated record remains GREEN. The user must see both facts, not a single red light.

29. Conformance and recoverability

GREEN means complete conformance. AMBER means the ZIP does not meet every requirement but preserves enough structure for partial verification or explicit reconstruction. RED means a serious contradiction, absence of the authoritative artefact, or processing risk.

PART VI · Security and reproducibility

30. Inspection before extraction

The verifier MUST read ZIP metadata before writing files to disk.

It MUST reject absolute paths, traversal, links, and normalised duplicates.

It MUST prevent one entry from overwriting another or a local file outside the temporary area.

It MUST NOT execute, actively preview, or decompress the internal source merely in order to verify the package.

It MUST treat the source as opaque bytes, even when it is another ZIP.

31. Encryption, signatures, and ZIP extensions

Package 2.0 MUST NOT be encrypted or password-protected.

It MUST NOT depend on multi-volume archives.

It MAY use ZIP64 when size requires it and the implementation supports it.

ZIP extra fields and timestamps are not part of cryptographic authority.

An external signature of the ZIP may coexist, but lies outside PKG-02.

32. Resource limits

Each implementation MUST apply configurable limits on number of entries, total uncompressed size, per-entry size, compression ratio, time, and memory. Exceeding a local limit does not by itself demonstrate that the package is malicious.

Case

Result

Local limit exceeded without a prohibited structural feature

AMBER — not fully processable on this device.

Impossible ZIP sizes or incoherent metadata

RED — malformed or unsafe package.

Extreme compression ratio

Abort before extraction; classify according to policy and available evidence.

Very large source stored without compression

May be verified through incremental reading if the implementation supports it.

33. ZIP reproducibility

The hash of the outer ZIP is not normative. Nevertheless, a generator SHOULD favour reproducible packages:

The outer ZIP hash MUST NOT be used as a package-conformance verdict or as a substitute for source_sha256 or the internal PTC hashes.

stable entry order;

stable paths and UTF-8 encoding;

controlled or documented timestamps;

stable compression policy;

absence of device-dependent extra data when unnecessary.

Two different ZIPs may be equivalent packages if they contain the same authoritative pieces and coherent derived pieces.

34. Source change during packaging

Where possible, the generator SHOULD obtain the source from a stable snapshot or check file size, identifier, and date before and after reading it. If it detects a change, it MUST NOT emit the package.

PART VII · Compatibility, publication, and repackaging

35. Earlier packages

A new Validator MUST continue generating and verifying earlier packages without requiring package_format_version 2.0. Absence of this field activates the legacy flow; it is not interpreted as a defective package 2.0.

36. Packages 2.0 in earlier validators

An earlier validator may not recognise manifest.json, FRS-01, or PUBLIC_NOTICE/. There is no guarantee of backward functional compatibility. The TXT pieces remain readable and preservable.

37. Repackaging

Extracting and recreating the ZIP does not automatically invalidate the declaration, source, or notice. The new container must be re-evaluated as a package; its internal axes are verified independently.

38. External ZIP hash

A repository MAY publish SHA-256, MD5, or another checksum of the ZIP as a download control. That value answers the identity of the container and does not replace source_sha256 or the PTC hashes.

39. Publication and URL

PKG-02 does not host the package or generate a public URL. When verification_url exists, it may already be active or be a reserved persistent address that will begin resolving after publication. Its availability is informative and does not alter the hashes.

45. Minimum interoperability tests

Two generators produce the same logical structure and the same authoritative pieces for PKG-V01.

Two verifiers emit the same four axes for all vectors.

Derived copies are compared byte-for-byte, not by normalised equality.

An incorrect manifest never corrects the integrated record.

Safe repackaging does not invalidate the internal pieces.

Hostile paths are rejected before writing to disk.

Safe additional entries produce AMBER and are never silently ignored.

Non-ASCII names without unambiguous UTF-8 signalling produce the same result in all verifiers.

Legacy packages follow their previous flow and are not forced into 2.0.

ANNEX A · The box is not the proof

This annex is informative. It summarises the difference between container integrity, authority of the pieces, and package conformance.

A.1 Four different questions

Layer

Question

External ZIP hash

Is it exactly the same distribution container?

PKG-02

Does the box contain the expected, safe, and coherent pieces?

FRS-01

Does the supplied source exactly match the bound source?

R-01/PTC-F

Do the canonical object and declaration remain intact?

A.2 Why a ZIP hash is not enough

A single ZIP hash detects any outer change, but does not distinguish whether the source, the PTC, a README, a timestamp, or the compression method changed. Nor does it survive extraction and repackaging of the same pieces.

A.3 Why redundancy is not an error

The integrated record is self-contained. Separate copies facilitate reading, import, preservation, and diagnosis. Their redundancy adds utility provided the specification states which is authoritative and how to detect a divergence.

A.4 Summary

Essential difference

PKG-02 does not turn the ZIP into a monolithic proof. It defines a governed box preserving an evidentiary structure capable of surviving repackaging and explaining exactly which piece matches, which is missing, and which contradicts the authority.

47. Resolution of hostile review v0.1

Claude Sonnet 5 confirmed the authority architecture and the four-axis model, but detected that PKG-V01 was not yet an executable vector: the declarative values and exact PTC serialisation were missing, and the physical ZIP was not distributed.

The exact logical values of all fields determining the PTC are published.

The complete ptc.txt and the exact rule for calculating hash_ptc_sha256 are published.

The descriptor, manifest.json, and README.txt are published with their physical termination.

The core pieces and fixture-specific auxiliary files are visually separated.

The physical PKG-V01 ZIP is distributed with a deterministic recipe and informative outer hash.

Absence of the integrated record remains package RED.

No malicious intent is inferred from an isolated technical symptom.

48. Resolution of the independent check on v0.2

DeepSeek reviewed PKG-02 v0.2 in blank context. Claude Sonnet 5 checked its five observations against the actual text and determined that two justified changes, two were already resolved, and one was incorrect.

An explicit coherence rule between public_notice and PUBLIC_NOTICE/ is incorporated.

It is reinforced that the outer ZIP hash is not a conformance verdict and does not replace the internal hashes.

The clarification that a source may itself be a ZIP is not repeated because it already appears literally in §13.

No further general reference to disclosure-notice-v1 is added because PKG-02 already states that it only transports it.

The informative character of media_type, already established in §21, is not modified.

PKG-V01 and all its preimages, sizes, and fingerprints remain unchanged.

49. Interoperability closure on v0.3

DeepSeek recommended freezing PKG-02 v0.3 and noted two details it considered non-blocking. GPT-5.6 Thinking assessed that both affected the possibility of two implementations emitting different results, and Manuel Barrera Anglada authorised their normative closure in v0.4.

The treatment of undeclared additional entries is defined: AMBER when they are safe and non-normative; RED when they are dangerous, ambiguous, or contradictory.

Unambiguous interpretation of entry names as UTF-8 is required, as is activation of the UTF-8 indicator for non-ASCII names.

Conformance states and vectors are added for both rules.

The authority model, four-axis architecture, and physical PKG-V01 vector are unchanged; with these additions, no normative issue remains pending for implementation of format 2.0.

50. Freezing criterion

Criterion met

PKG-02 v0.4 is technically frozen. The specification contains sufficient authority, structure, grammar, security, recovery, states, and vectors for two independent implementations to generate, locate, verify, reconstruct, and diagnose packages without interpreting the drafter's intent or collapsing the four result axes. Any subsequent normative change will require a new version.
